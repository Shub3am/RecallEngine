# Lumen Cloud support handbook

## Refunds

Customers on monthly plans can request a full refund within 14 days of their first payment.
Annual plans are refundable pro rata for the unused months. Refunds go back to the original
payment method and usually arrive within 5 to 7 business days.

## Escalation

Priority 1 incidents (production down) are paged to the on-call engineer immediately.
Priority 2 incidents get a first response within 4 business hours.
